DESIGN A: THE WHOLE SCREEN IS THE PARK

Idea, in three sentences:
There is no card: the isometric pixel park of the approved drafts fills the screen from edge to edge at 2x, and the
interface is only a light frame around it (the logo on a ribbon plate at the top, the menu at the bottom).
The picture itself tells the game: the warm Thrill side (drop tower, wooden coaster, swinging ship, kart track, orange
stalls, autumn trees) lies under the word "Thrills", and the cool Chill side (big wheel, carousel, pond with swan
boats, bandstand on a lawn, teal and blue stalls, green trees) lies under the word "Chills"; a plaza with a fountain
and a round flower bed in two halves (orange and blue) joins them. On the phone the same park is drawn again in
portrait, not shrunk: Thrill at the top, Chill below, and the calm pond lies behind the menu.

Where the New game / Load / Credits panel opens:
Desktop (1280 x 800): a plate about 440 px wide in the centre, between the logo plate and the menu row
(x 420 to 860, y 172 to 692). It covers the plaza, the flower bed and the four stalls; the tower, the coaster hills
and the ship stay in view on its left, the wheel, the carousel and the pond on its right. The logo stays above it and
the menu row stays below it.
Phone (390 x 844): a sheet that rises from the bottom edge and takes the place of the menu (y 300 to 844, full width
with a 12 px margin). It covers the wheel, the carousel and the pond; the logo, the tower and the coaster stay in
view above it.

Pictures (written by make.py, palette of the drafts, 46 colours each, shown at 2x with hard pixels):
park-desktop.png  640 x 400 art pixels -> 1280 x 800.
park-phone.png    195 x 422 art pixels -> 390 x 844. A second composition made by the same code.
Nothing else is a picture. No mascots; guests are 4 to 6 art pixels high, carousel horses and swan boats 6.

HTML and CSS (base.css of the kit plus the <style> of intro.html):
- The logo: Fredoka 700 text on a white .plate; "Thrills" in --accent-thrill, "&" in ink, "Chills" in --accent-chill,
  each word with an ink outline (-webkit-text-stroke with paint-order: stroke fill) and a hard ink shadow.
- The ribbon tails left and right of the plate (orange for Thrill, blue for Chill): two divs with clip-path.
- The tagline, with "thrill seekers" and "chill seekers" in the two logo colours.
- The six menu items, all .btn: Continue (.btn.primary, blue, with the name of the saved park as a second line; that
  line is optional), New game (gold), then Load, Import, Settings, Credits (pale, smaller).
  Desktop: one row along the bottom, 68 px and 48 px high, 12 px apart.
  Phone: Continue and New game across the full width (64 and 56 px), then one row of four (48 px), all in the lower
  quarter of the screen.

Least happy with: the guests are so small (the brief allows 6 pixels) that the avenues look less alive than in the
drafts, and the lower strip of the desktop picture is kept plain on purpose so that the menu row sits on calm grass.
