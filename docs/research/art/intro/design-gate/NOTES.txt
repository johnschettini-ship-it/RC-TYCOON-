DESIGN C: THE PARK ENTRANCE

Idea, in three sentences:
The whole screen is a front view of the park gate: two towers (teal roof on the Chill side, orange roof on the Thrill
side) carry a striped fan arch with bulbs and flags and a big marquee sign board with the name of the game in pixel
lettering. Behind the gate the two sides of the park peek over under a split sky: on the left the big wheel, pines and a
garden pond under a cool sky, on the right the wooden coaster, the drop tower and autumn trees under a warm sky, and a
sandy path with tiny guests leads from the bottom of the screen through the gate to a fountain. The menu is part of the
entrance: each tower stands on a ticket booth with a striped awning, and the booth carries three hanging sign boards:
Continue, Load and Import on the Chill booth (come back to a saved park), New game, Settings and Credits on the Thrill
booth (start something new).

Where the New game / Load / Credits panel opens:
Desktop (1280 x 800): in the gateway, between the two booths and under the sign board: a plate up to 480 px wide,
  centred at x = 640, from y = 276 (it takes the place of the tagline plate) down to about y = 770. It covers only the
  view through the gate (path, fountain, balloons); the sign with the logo and both booths with the menu stay visible.
Phone (390 x 844): a sheet that rises from the bottom edge to y = 348, just under the sign board (it covers the tagline
  plate, the view through the gate and the two booths). The sign with the logo and the rides above it stay visible.

Pictures (written by make.py; pixel art, kit palette, 47 and 45 colours, shown at 2x with hard pixels):
scene-desktop.png, 640 x 400 art pixels, shown at 1280 x 800: sky, rides, park view, the gate with towers, fan arch,
  sign board and the logo "Thrills & Chills" in pixel lettering (1-bit glyphs of Fredoka 700, bevelled, ink outline and
  a hard ink shadow), the two ticket booths with their awnings and the straps of the boards, path, pond, flower bed, guests.
scene-phone.png, 195 x 422 art pixels, shown at 390 x 844: its own composition, not a crop: the logo stands in two
  lines on a tall sign board, the wheel, the coaster and the drop tower peek over the arch, the gate opening in the
  middle shows the path to the fountain, and the two booths meet at the bottom with a narrow lane between them.

HTML and CSS (base.css of the kit plus the <style> of intro.html):
The six menu items: .btn sign boards on the booth walls (Continue .btn.primary in Chill blue and New game in gold, both
  60 px high on desktop and 56 px on the phone; Load, Import, Settings, Credits 48 px, white). On the phone they form
  two columns of three in the lower third of the screen (y = 628 to 808), in reach of a thumb.
The tagline: a small cream plate (3 px ink outline, hard shadow) that hangs on two chains under the sign board.
A hidden <h1> with the name of the game for screen readers (the visible logo is part of the picture).

Notes for a build: the interface parts sit on the picture's 2 px grid inside a stage of the picture's size
(1280 x 800 centred, or 390 x 844). For other window sizes the stage stays centred and the sky and the lawn of the
picture are extended to the sides; the logo, the arch bulbs and the flags can be cut out as their own sprites to animate.
