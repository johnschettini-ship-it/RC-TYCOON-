DESIGN E: THE SKYLINE AT ORANGE DUSK

Idea, in three sentences:
The whole screen is one calm evening sky over the park seen from the side, low on the screen: the wooden coaster and the
drop tower stand on the warm Thrill side at the left in front of the low sun, the carousel and the big wheel stand on the
cool Chill side at the right over a lake, under a teal sky with the moon and the first stars, and the sky runs between
them in flat bands from orange to teal.
The name of the game stands in the sky as large block lettering, and each word stands over its own side: "Thrills" in
orange over the coaster, "&" in ink over the middle, "Chills" in blue over the wheel.
The menu is a plain white plate: a column at the left on the desktop, a compact block at the bottom of the phone under
the skyline.

Where the New game / Load / Credits panel opens:
Desktop: a plate 440 px wide directly to the right of the menu plate (x 344 to 784, y 268 to 756): it grows out of the
menu like a second column and covers the coaster and the tower; the logo, the tagline, the carousel, the wheel and the
lake stay visible, and the menu stays in reach to switch between New game, Load and Credits.
Phone: a sheet that rises from the bottom edge to y = 316 (just under the logo): it takes the place of the menu block and
covers the skyline; the logo stays visible above it, and the sheet's buttons sit at its bottom in reach of the thumb.

Pictures (written by make.py; all shown at 2x with hard pixels, palette colours only):
scene-desktop.png  640 x 400 art pixels -> 1280 x 800. Sky bands, sun, moon, stars, clouds, two balloons, far hills and
                   woods, coaster with station, tower, stall, carousel, wheel, fence, lamps with glows, promenade with
                   tiny guests (4 to 6 pixels high), a path, flower beds, fountain, near trees, lake with pier and boats.
                   45 colours. The menu plate covers art x 20 to 164, y 170 to 380: only lawn, autumn trees and the far
                   end of the coaster lie behind it.
scene-phone.png    195 x 422 art pixels -> 390 x 844. The same park composed again for the portrait screen: the rides
                   stand closer and overlap in depth, the ground below the promenade is plain lawn (the menu block
                   covers everything below art y = 274). 41 colours.
logo-desktop.png   520 x 72 -> 1040 x 144. One line of block lettering.
logo-phone.png     187 x 142 -> 374 x 284. Two lines, a small "&" between two rules.
The lettering is made by make.py from the game's own font Fredoka (weight 700, read from the repository, nothing is
written there): each word becomes a hard 1-bit mask, then a face of three flat tones (the light comes from the upper
left), a side to the lower right and 1-pixel ink lines. No anti-aliasing.

HTML and CSS (base.css of the kit plus the <style> of intro.html):
The <h1> holds the logo picture (alt text "Thrills & Chills"). The tagline is HTML text on a small pill plate under the
logo (desktop only; the phone leaves it out to keep the logo and the skyline large). The menu is a .plate with six .btn:
Continue (.btn.primary, with the name of the saved park as a second line), New game (gold), then Load, Import, Settings,
Credits. Desktop: one column, 288 px wide, at the left. Phone: Continue and New game across the full width, the other
four in a grid of two by two; every item lies in the lower third of the screen.

For the real game: the scene picture would be anchored to the bottom centre at a whole-number scale and the flat sky
colours would fill the rest; the logo and the menu are placed by CSS, so they follow the size of the window. Without a
saved park, "Continue" is left out and "New game" becomes the primary button.
