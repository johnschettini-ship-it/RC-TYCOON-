DESIGN B: TWO HALVES, ONE PARK

Idea, in three sentences:
The whole screen is one pixel picture of one park, cut by a slanted cream seam into a warm Thrill half (gold sky, a wooden
coaster at the top of its drop, a drop tower) and a cool Chill half (blue sky, a big wheel, a carousel, pines, a lake).
The name is large pixel lettering that lies across the seam: "Thrills" in orange on the warm half, "Chills" in blue on
the cool half, and the "&" sits on the seam itself. One paved path with lamps and tiny guests runs through both halves,
so it reads as one park with two moods; the menu stands where the two halves meet.

Where the New game / Load / Credits panel opens:
Desktop: in the middle column, in the place of the menu plate (it may grow to 440 px wide, from y = 240 to y = 760).
  The logo stays above it; the coaster and the tower stay visible on its left, the wheel on its right.
Phone: a sheet that rises from the bottom edge over the menu and the lake, up to just under the word "Chills"
  (about the lower 55 % of the screen). The logo and the Thrill scene stay visible above it.

Layout:
Desktop 1280 x 800: seam from the top (x = 664) to the bottom (x = 464), one art pixel to the left every four rows; the logo in one line at the top, tagline pill
  under it, menu plate 360 px wide in the centre (Continue, New game, then Load / Import / Settings / Credits in a 2 x 2 grid).
Phone 390 x 844: the halves stack, Thrill on top and Chill below, with the same seam rising gently to the right; the logo
  stacks as "Thrills" / "&" / "Chills" across it; the menu stands on the calm lake in the lower quarter (Continue, New game,
  then the four small items in one row). The tagline is left out on the phone.

Pictures (written by make.py, all shown at 2x with hard pixels):

logo-p-thrills.png, logo-p-and.png, logo-p-chills.png: the same lettering for the phone (48 and 36 art pixels).

HTML and CSS (base.css of the kit plus the <style> of intro.html):
The tagline pill (.plate), the menu plate (.plate) with its two-colour top stripe, the six buttons (.btn): Continue in
the Chill blue with the name of the saved park, New game in warm gold, the four others pale. The stripe on the
plate is split at the same slant and place as the seam, so the seam seems to run on through the plate. The logo pictures are placed by
CSS on the 2 px art grid; the <h1> carries the name as text for screen readers.

In the game: the scene would be anchored to the centre of the screen and cropped at the edges; the logo and the menu
are anchored to the seam.
