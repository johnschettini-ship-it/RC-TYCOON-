DESIGN D: THE TICKET

Idea, in three sentences:
The intro is one big paper admission ticket in an orange evening sky: the name of the game is printed large on its main
part, and the six menu items are coupons on its tear-off stub under the words "Admit one".
The ticket itself tells the idea of the game: its striped border, its two round badges (a bolt and a snowflake) and the
lettering are warm orange on the Thrill half and cool blue on the Chill half.
Low on the screen, behind the ticket, one calm strip shows the park at sunset: the drop tower and the wooden coaster on
the left, the carousel, the big wheel and a pond on the right, and the sun going down between the two sides.

Where the New game / Load / Credits panel opens:
Desktop: in the place of the stub. A plate up to 400 px wide from x = 840 to x = 1240, with its top at the top edge of
  the ticket (y = 52), growing downwards as far as y = 760. It covers the menu, the balloon and part of the wheel; the
  name on the main part of the ticket stays in full view on its left. (A 440 px plate would touch the last letter.)
Phone: a sheet that rises from the bottom edge up to the tear line of the ticket (y = 384). It covers the stub with the
  menu and the skyline (the lower 55 % of the screen); the upper part of the ticket with the name stays in view.

Layout:
Desktop 1280 x 800: the ticket lies in the upper middle (1056 x 424 px at 112, 52). Main part 744 px wide: small print,
  the name in one line (652 x 84 px), the tagline, a footer row "Thrill side / Good for all rides / Chill side" between
  the two badges. Stub 312 px wide behind the tear line: "Admit one", Continue (64 px high, with the name of the saved
  park), New game (56 px), then Load, Import, Settings and Credits in a 2 x 2 grid (48 px), a barcode. The skyline
  takes the lowest 320 px, under the ticket; the sky above it stays flat.
Phone 390 x 844: the ticket stands upright (350 x 648 px at 20, 12). Upper part: the name in three lines ("Thrills" in
  an orange frame, "&" between the two badges, "Chills" in a blue frame), the tagline in two lines. Lower part, below
  the tear line and in the lower half of the screen: the same six coupons, all in reach of a thumb. The skyline is a
  different, smaller picture (tower, first hill of the coaster, carousel, wheel) in the 180 px under the ticket.
Other window sizes (not shown): the sky is flat bands, so the top band and the lowest band can grow; the skyline stays
  at the bottom edge and the ticket stays centred in the sky above it.

HTML and CSS (base.css of the kit plus the <style> of intro.html):
The tagline and all small print on the ticket ("Park pass", "No. 000128", "Thrill side", "Good for all rides",
"Chill side", "Admit one", the serial number) are HTML text in Fredoka, so they can be translated.
The six menu items are .btn buttons of the game's interface (3 px ink outline, hard shadow, round corners): Continue is
.btn.primary, New game is gold, the four small ones are white. Each has a coupon notch on its left and right side:
a ::before and ::after circle with the paper colour and an ink border, cut in half with clip-path.
The <h1> holds the lettering picture with the alt text "Thrills & Chills".

Motion ideas for later (not in the mock-up): the balloon drifts, the wheel and the coaster trains move slowly, a pressed
coupon dips by its 4 px shadow like every button of the game.

No characters: no mascots; the only people are guests of 4 to 5 art pixels and riders of 3 art pixels.
