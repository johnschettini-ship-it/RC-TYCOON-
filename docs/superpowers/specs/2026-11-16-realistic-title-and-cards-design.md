# Realistic title screen and cards — design

- **Status:** draft for owner approval (2026-11-16).
- **Owner request:** "we need to rework the title and cards"; "Should match" (the realistic look); "Match the realistic look" (picked the full match: title, card frames and tool cards).
- **Related:** `phase-7-title-screen.md` (design E: layout rules G1–G15, menu, panel), Phase 7 decks (`src/ui/decks/`), `phase-8c-realistic-art.md` (kit, bake, originality §8, E2: the website keeps today's look).

## Intent

In the realistic build the park looks real, but the title screen is a pixel-art skyline and the cards use today's flat frames and flat tool icons. The first screen and the cards on screen all the time must look like the same game as the park.

## Goals

1. **Title screen (realistic build):** a baked realistic park panorama replaces the pixel skyline, with the same layout, name position, menu and panel behaviour (design E rules G1–G15 hold).
2. **Card frames (realistic build):** ride, stall, scenery and tool cards get frames that suit the realistic art.
3. **Tool cards (realistic build):** the 7 tool cards (footpath, queue, staff, research, marketing, finance, park) show small baked realistic objects instead of flat icons.
4. Everything is made by code and baked in the existing pipeline; nothing downloaded, traced or generated by AI (spec §8).

## Non-goals

- The website build (E2): it keeps design E and today's cards unchanged.
- Other interface parts (top bar, mood bar, deck chips, panels, tips) — unchanged in this change.
- New title menu items or flows.

## Anti-goals

- The title takes longer to appear than today (no waiting on art before the menu shows).
- Title art stays in memory during play (it must be freed when the park opens).
- Text on cards becomes harder to read (contrast, size) than today.
- Any picture copies an existing game's look, logo or park.

## Approaches considered (owner picked 1)

1. **Full match (picked):** baked panorama title + new card frames + baked tool objects.
2. Title and frames only; tool cards keep restyled flat icons.
3. Title only.

## Decisions (controller's calls, owner may correct)

**Title**
- **Scene:** an original park at golden hour seen from slightly above: a lake in front, a winding path with guests, the big wheel, the swing tower and a coaster hill behind, low hills and sky. Built from the batch 1 ride models and the kit; baked in two framings like design E: wide (desktop) and tall (phone).
- **Layers and motion:** three layers (sky, park, foreground trees and water) with a slow parallax drift of a few pixels, and the big wheel turning in a short baked loop (8 pictures). On `prefers-reduced-motion`, no drift and a still wheel.
- **Name:** "Thrills & Chills" as a baked sign made in the kit: painted wood board, lit letters in the vendored Fredoka font, the same two layouts as design E (one line on wide screens, three lines on phones). Same positions as the design E logo.
- **Loading:** the title shows at once with a sky gradient and the menu; the panorama fades in when decoded (target under 1 s on the owner's phone). Title pictures are released when a park opens; they are not part of the 128 MB play budget, but are freed before it starts.
- **Size:** the title art adds at most 3 MB compressed to the app (S5).

**Cards**
- **Frame:** a card face with a soft drop shadow, a thin warm-cream edge, rounded corners as today, the picture filling the top, and the name and price on a dark translucent band at the bottom (white text, contrast ≥ 4.5:1). Category colours (rides, stalls, scenery, tools) kept as a narrow top stripe so decks stay recognisable.
- **Tool objects:** footpath — a short stretch of the paving tiles; queue — rope posts with a rope; staff — a mechanic figure with a toolbox; research — a drafting table with a ride model; marketing — a poster board; finance — a ticket booth with a cash box; park — the park entrance arch. Baked like card pictures (one set, density 2), framed the same way.
- **Selected/disabled states:** same rules as today (selected: lifted with a brighter edge; unaffordable: dimmed picture, price in the warning colour).

## Builder-check answers (controller's calls)

1. **Title layout:** the realistic build gets its own layout module, re-derived for the panorama (non-integer smooth scaling, its own anchor rows for the name-fit and menu rules, a gradient fill below the picture). It keeps design E's intent — name above the rides, menu not covering the wheel, safe areas — and design E's tests stay for the website build; the new module gets equivalent tests.
2. **Delivery:** the title pictures are WebP files shipped in the realistic build's art folder (baked art is already image files there), loaded with `createImageBitmap` outside the set manager. They are decoded only while the title shows and closed when a park opens, before the play budget is used.
3. **Sizes:** layers are baked at the screen's needed density, capped at 2 device px per CSS px (phone render rule). Wide framing 1920×800 px at most, tall 900×1300 at most, per layer; decoded total for the title ≤ 24 MB. The wheel is a separate small sprite (8 frames) on the park layer, not full-screen frames. Compressed total ≤ 3 MB.
4. **Return to the title:** if the game returns to the title, the park's sets are released first (the park is closed), then the title art is decoded again; the sky gradient and menu show at once each time.
5. **Cards:** all 8 base tool cards get a baked object — add `pathItems`: a bench with a lamp. Extension cards with no baked picture keep their icon inside the new frame (same frame, icon centred on the picture area).
6. **Tool objects path:** the tool objects go in the same `preview:cards` set under ids `tool:<id>`. The 8 objects are new kit models (in scope); the set grows by about 8 small pictures (≈ 0.5 MB decoded).
7. **Card layout:** the name/price band overlays the bottom of the picture (intended). Text sizes unchanged; tool cards show only the name in the band. Re-run the fit probe (812×375, large text) and the a11y checks.
8. **Name sign:** the name never translates ("Thrills & Chills" is the game's name). The sign is a picture; the title keeps a visually hidden heading with the name for screen readers.
9. **Website byte-identity:** checked as today (build the website at base and head, compare JS/CSS/HTML). Realistic-only code and CSS live in modules and a stylesheet only the realistic entry imports; shared files (`title.ts`, `card.ts`) may gain a call into a realistic hook only behind the compile-time `VITE_ART === 'real'` guard, which folds away in the website build.

## Constraints

- Realistic code and styles only in the realistic build (`VITE_ART === 'real'` at compile time, or a stylesheet loaded only there); website build byte-identical.
- Design E layout rules G1–G15 and the a11y checks of the title and the decks still pass; card text sizes unchanged.
- Fits a 390×844 phone and a phone held sideways (the layout fixes already made).
- Storage under `tcreal:`.

## Left to the builder

- Exact camera framing of the panorama and the sign, judged on screenshots by the owner.
- How the stylesheet is swapped in the realistic build.
- Card frame pixel values beyond the rules above.
